## Editorial principles applied

Repairs were limited to:

- awkward literal English syntax;
- grammar and clause structure;
- clearer idiomatic sentence flow;
- dialogue readability;
- removal of redundant or unnatural English wording where the same Tamil meaning remains fully represented;
- smoother English presentation of source metaphors without replacing them;
- preservation of source rhetoric, repetition, exclamation and information-release order.

The review did **not**:

- add or remove a literary/display block;
- reorder source information;
- alter a provenance comment;
- normalize deliberate Tamil name/spacing variants;
- add historical, political, religious or cultural explanation;
- fact-correct source-framed claims;
- alter canonical or assembled Tamil;
- cross the pending 132→133 boundary.
