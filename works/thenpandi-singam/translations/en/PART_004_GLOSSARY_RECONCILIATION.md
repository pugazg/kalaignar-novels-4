- `வாளுக்கு வேலி` → **Vaalukku Veli**

## Colonel Durai / Agniyu reconciliation

Earlier Part004 sections use the source title **Colonel Durai**.

E16 explicitly states in the Tamil narrative that the officer respectfully called `கர்னல் துரை` is the man named `அக்னியூ`.

Therefore:

- `கர்னல் துரை` → **Colonel Durai**
- `அக்னியூ` → **Agniyu**
- `கர்னல் அக்னியூ` → **Colonel Agniyu**

These are source-supported co-referential forms and are not globally normalized to a single English label.

No outside historical identity or standardized spelling is introduced.
