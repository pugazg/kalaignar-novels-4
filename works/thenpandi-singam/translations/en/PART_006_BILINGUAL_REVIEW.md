### E21 / section26 — 4

1. Source metaphor `விளம்பரப்படுத்திக் கொண்டிருந்தன` had been softened during editorial review.

   Corrected to:

   **`Kalyani’s face brightened, though she was perplexed about how to begin the lessons for the first time while her brother was away.`**

4. Source `அண்ணன் ஊரிலிருந்து வந்துவிட்டுமே!` explicitly retains the brother’s return-from-away context.

   Corrected to:

   **`Let Anna return to town first!`**

### E22 / section27 — 4

1. Source metaphor `நினைவாற்றலைச் சாணை பிடித்தான்` was flattened to “searched his memory”.

   Restored as:

   **`Adappan sharpened his memory!`**

2. Source `அவளைக் கொத்துவதற்குத் துடித்தன` uses the image of pecking.

   Corrected from generic “tear into her” to:

   **`seemed eager to peck at her`**

   Restored in English:

   **`didn’t they drive you away from the Paganeri house when you went asking for a bride, telling you not even to climb its steps?`**

4. Editorial compression had reduced the source’s fourfold rhetorical sequence `அழுதும் புலம்பியும் கண்ணீர் விட்டும் கதறியும்`.

   Restored as:

   **`weeps, laments, sheds tears and cries out`**

### E23 / section28 — 4

   **`The song was a collection of pasurams...`**

   **`An Ambalakkarar had a certain status and pomp to maintain!`**

4. Source `தனது இருவிழிகளால்` explicitly marks both eyes.

   Restored as:

   **`Vaalukkuveli stood there, drinking in that beautiful form with both eyes!`**
