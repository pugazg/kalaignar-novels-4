#### Correction 1 — Adappan's care for departing guests

Tamil:
- `அவர்களையெல்லாம் அன்புடன் கவனித்து ... தானே முன்னின்று கருத்த ஆதப்பன் தனி அக்கறை எடுத்துக் கொண்டான்.`

Pre-bilingual English:
- `Karutha Adappan personally attended to each of them, seating them in carts and sending them to their lodgings.`

Maintained English:
- **`Karutha Adappan personally took special care to look after each of them, seating them in carts and sending them to their lodgings.`**

Reason:
- the shorter editorial wording dropped the explicit source idea of **special care** (`தனி அக்கறை`);
- the maintained English restores that source emphasis without adding new information.

#### Correction 2 — Kalyani Nachiyar sibling-reference clarity

Tamil:
- `பாகனேரி அம்பலக்காரன் வாளுக்குவேலித் தேவன் தனது உயிருக்கு மேலாக நேசிக்கிற உடன்பிறப்பு கல்யாணி நாச்சியாரை ...`

Pre-bilingual English:
- `Urangaappuli once sent word asking for the hand of Kalyani Nachiyar—his own sister, whom Paganeri Ambalakkarar Vaalukkuveli Thevar loves more than his life!`

Maintained English:
- **`Urangaappuli once sent word asking for the hand of Kalyani Nachiyar—the sister whom Paganeri Ambalakkarar Vaalukkuveli Thevar loves more than his own life!`**

Reason:
- the pre-bilingual pronoun `his own sister` could grammatically attach to Urangaappuli and therefore introduced relationship ambiguity;
- Tamil clearly identifies Kalyani as the sibling loved by Vaalukkuveli;
- the maintained wording removes the ambiguity without adding outside relationship information.
