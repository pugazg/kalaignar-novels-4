## Further English-only fidelity corrections

Six additional English-only corrections were required.

### section18 / E13 — 1

Source:

`தலைவலி போய் திருகு வலி வந்துடாம நல்லவேளை தப்பிச்சேன்.`

The English was grammatically awkward after editorial review. It was tightened to:

**`Good thing I escaped without one headache giving way to a screw-pain.`**

This preserves the source image without substituting an unrelated English proverb.

### section19 / E14 — 2

1. Source idiom:

   `தலைக்கு மேல் போய்விட்டது! இனியென்ன அச்சம்?`

   Literal **`It has gone over our heads`** can misleadingly imply failure to understand in English. It was corrected to:

   **`Things have gone beyond control! What fear is left now?`**

2. Source:

   `அந்த மகிழ்ச்சிக்கிடையிலேயும் ஒரு மனப்புயலோடு வாளுக்குவேலியும் ஆதப்பனும்...`

   The English had singular **`his mind`** even though both Vaalukkuveli and Adappan are the grammatical subjects. It was corrected to:

   **`carrying a storm in their minds`**

### section20 / E15 — 2

1. Source:

   `எப்படி என் போர்த்தந்திரம்!`

   The English had dropped `போர்` and rendered only **strategy**. It was corrected to:

   **`How about my war strategy!`**

2. Source:

   `இடையில் இருந்த கட்டாரி கைக்கு வந்து விட்டது!`

   The editorial wording **`was already in his hand`** introduced an unnecessary prior-time implication. It was corrected to:

   **`The dagger at his waist was now in his hand!`**

### section21 / E16 — 1

Source:

`பட்டமங்கலத்தார்தான் இன்னும் எதிலும் பட்டுக் கொள்ளாமல் இருக்கிறார்.`

The English had **`Pattamangalam alone`**, which could be read as the place rather than the source’s honorific human referent. It was corrected to:

**`The Pattamangalam man alone has so far kept out of everything.`**

The following sentence’s `he and his younger brother Vairamuthan` now retains clear referential continuity.
