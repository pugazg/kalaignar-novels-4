# 18

“Because our Nachiyaramma learned Bharatanatyam when she was a child, she picks up everything Vadivu teaches her at once,” Lalithangi said with a coaxing laugh, praising and congratulating Kalyani Nachiyar.

“And the teacher-amma is clever too, isn’t she!” Kaadai and Kolari praised Vadivambal in unison.

Vadivu, however, was yearning because she had not been able to meet Vaalukku Veli! And news arrived right there that turned that yearning into disappointment.

Some of the servants in Vaalukkuveli’s mansion hurried inside and took several of the silver vessels that had been beautifully arranged in a room adjoining the hall and placed them in the verandah.

Bronze vessels, brass pots, copper vessels and other costly articles were also brought out of the room and piled in the verandah. Though the room was not completely—

<!-- source boundary: scan 155 → scan 156 -->

—empty, nearly half its contents had been brought outside. The servants seemed to be in a great bustle.

Vadivambal looked at Lalithangi and Nathamuni in mounting wonder. Kalyani Nachiyar called Kaadai and Kolari over, whispered something into their ears, and kept watching as the two went toward the room off the hall.

Lalithangi gently nipped Vadivu’s ear and whispered, “It must be the Ambalakkarar’s order. He must have decided to send vessels and household goods like these as gifts because he is happy that you have come to teach dance to his younger sister.”

Joy overflowed in Vadivu when she heard that! Nathamuni, who had overheard Lalithangi’s words to Vadivambal with his snake-like ears, moved closer to offer them an explanation. By then Kalyani Nachiyar had gone into her room to change out of her Bharatanatyam costume and into another outfit.

“Don’t you know the custom among Ambalakkarars? If one of them develops a desire for a woman from our artistic profession and decides to live with her, the first thing he does is send seer-varisai like this! Vadivambal has been struck by a great stroke of luck!”

When Nathamuni announced his discovery, Vadivu sank into delight, though one doubt still troubled her.

<!-- source boundary: scan 156 → scan 157 -->

“The custom may be like that! But shouldn’t all these customs take place only after asking the woman for her consent and after she says yes?” she asked Nathamuni.

Lalithangi immediately cut in. “What, amma? You don’t consent? Then say so! I’ll tell those men right now to stop everything,” she teased mischievously.

“No! No! Don’t you go barging in and creating some other confusion!” Vadivu said with a shy smile.

‘What I wished for is going to happen! Vaalukkuveli, tall in stature and strong-armed, will embrace my slender waist, drink from my cheek-petals, and satisfy the hunger of the soft bed! He will worship me with wealth! He will give me an honour respected by many!’

Vadivu’s thoughts spun onward in circles of fantasy!

Just then Kaadai and Kolari bounded into Kalyani’s room in exuberant excitement.

“Amma! Very good news! Do you know where our Ayya is sending all this seer-varisai?”

“I sent you to find that out, didn’t I? Why are you coming back and asking me?”

“We found out, amma! The seer-varisai is going to Sundarambal’s house in Tirukkottiyur!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 157 → scan 158 -->

“Is that so?” Kalyani Nachiyar’s eyebrows became question marks, yet a smile blossomed at the edge of her lips!

“Do you know why he is sending the seer-varisai, amma?”

“Why?”

“Hmm! You ask as though you don’t know! It means our Ayya has entered into a Gandharva marriage with that Sundari!”

Kalyani felt as though spring and a cold north wind were blowing over her at the same time! Though a gentle breeze seemed to be entering her brother’s life, she could not keep herself from thinking of the immense storm through which he was moving.

Another anxiety troubled her: what would Adappan think of this sudden decision her brother had made? Whatever the case, she was also embraced by the consolation that it was enough if her beloved brother was happy.

Kaadai and Kolari came out of the room and blurted out the truth in front of Vadivambal and her companions. Vadivambal stood there with her heart shattered into a thousand pieces!

Like a mad elephant breaking its chain and charging wildly, the jealousy in her heart charged at and crashed into her elder sister Sundari!

<!-- source boundary: scan 158 → scan 159 -->

She seethed as though her own sister had snatched away from her an object already stamped as belonging to Vadivu!

Can the blaze of a brick kiln be hidden by throwing a thin cloth over it? That was Vadivu’s condition there! Yet she was a clever woman. She guarded herself carefully so that the spear-thrusts and sword-strokes raging on the battlefield of her heart would not show in the expressions of her face.

Swearing to herself that one day, no matter when, she would carry out exactly what she had resolved and would certainly succeed, she told Kaadai and Kolari that she was waiting to take leave of Kalyani Nachiyar. Before they could run inside, Kalyani herself came there!

“Everything has ended very well. The clash that arose between your brother and my elder sister has now ended in love. Do you know how sweet it is for me to hear this? It is God’s will: one day it makes some people enemies! The next day it turns those very people into affectionate lovers!”

There was no reason for Kalyani Nachiyar to know that these words came from Vadivambal’s lips alone while her heart lay withered. Smiling, she expressed her wish that Vadivambal should cooperate so that her dance training could continue!
