# How the Story Was Born!

My younger brother Thennarasu, who gives flavour to words and creates treasuries of stories, is highly skilled at giving the form of a short story or a long story to true events of the past as well as the present. His book *Semmathulai* is only ninety pages long, yet it is a collection of feeling. With the help of a few notes found in *Kallar Sarithiram*, written by N. M. Venkatasami Nattar—whom the Tamil world of letters called “Nattar Ayya”—and by gathering old accounts heard by word of mouth, he portrayed in that short story how Paganeri Nadu and Pattamangalam Nadu, in the Sivaganga and Tiruppattur regions of Ramanathapuram, clashed, nursed enmity, and shed blood. I wished to turn that story, against the historical background of Tamilakam's freedom fighters, into a treasury of imagination in which valour, love, and affection surge forth. The result of that wish was *Thenpandi Singam*, which appeared as a serial in *Kungumam* and won the appreciation of many. Abirami Publications wished to publish it in expanded book form. My thanks to my friend Ramanathan of Thirumagal Nilayam, who obtained permission from Tamilkkani Pathippagam, which holds the rights to this book, brought out the fourth edition, and has placed it in your hands! And now, you are about to meet Thenpandi Singam.

15-5-90  
Chennai.

Affectionately,  
M. Karunanidhi
