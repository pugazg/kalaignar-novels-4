# 7

“Some people from Pattamangalam nadu, at the lodging where they are staying, were apparently saying: ‘From advising on village problems and looking after official affairs all the way to the storeroom and kitchen, a man of an untouchable caste moves about freely there; are we to go and eat a feast in that house?’ That is what I meant.”

On hearing this explanation from Meganathan, Vaalukku Veli stroked his moustache and fell silent! Kalyani too stood motionless, the very image of silence!

“I am not the sort to plead, ‘They must eat this feast somehow! Only if their footprints fall in the Paganeri Ambalakkarar’s house will we be blessed!’ So I went to the Pattamangalam men and spoke to them firmly and bluntly.”

Karutha Adappan came in growling these words in a loud voice.

“All right, then. They are not going to come!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 55 → scan 56 -->

—Kalyani came to that conclusion! A cloud of sorrow veiled her full-moon face! Eager to learn the whole story, she stayed right there.

“What happened, Adappa?”

“Exactly what Meganathan said, Anna! The moment I heard it, I went myself to the lodging where they had been put up!”

“Tell it in detail! And tell it quickly too!”

“I’ll tell you! ‘People of Pattamangalam! You are older than I am! I am only a young man! So don’t dismiss whatever I say as the rash talk of youth! We believe in God! We say that we submit ourselves to Him! We proclaim that humankind itself is His creation. Is it just, then, to divide that creation into high and low—to divide it into classes—to follow doctrines that say one person is worthy of worship while another is not even fit to worship?

“‘Leave even those questions of justice and injustice aside! You do not accept that Meganathan is treated in our house as an equal, as a friend, like one born with us. If word spreads that this is why you refused to come to the feast, do you know how great a caste riot that news could sow the seeds of? Have you understood the consequences?

<!-- source boundary: scan 56 → scan 57 -->

“‘Will the world fail to say that the decision taken by the Pattamangalam people was the cause of that terrible turmoil?’ Anna! When I piled question upon question like this, Ambalakkarar Vallatharayan shut his mouth and became silent! Then Vairamuthan’s voice rang out there in support of me!”

A glow like the eastern dawn slowly rose over Kalyani Nachiyar’s face!

“Vairamuthan’s arguments supported me! They were stronger than the points I had made. Vairamuthan said firmly and clearly that Karutha Adappan’s words should not be brushed aside!”

Was that Kalyani Nachiyar’s face—or the radiance of seven suns? Was it the same face that, only a short while ago, had been of two minds?

“Vairamutha! When have I ever acted against you? Even so, is it so easy to think of the highs and lows that have existed since our ancestors’ time as ordinary mounds and hollows and level them with a spade?”

“Anna! Sometimes even a river in flood levels the mounds and hollows in the earth! We must not create a situation in which a river of blood has to swell in order to level the caste and religious mounds and hollows of humankind, must we?”

“All right, Thambi Vairamutha! As you say, we will go to the feast at the Paganeri house!”

<!-- source boundary: scan 57 → scan 58 -->

Vaalukku Veli slapped his younger brother Adappan on the back and laughed heartily. “Sabash! You and Vairamuthan together have managed to bend Vallatharayan to your will!”

There could be nothing surprising in Vairamuthan’s eagerness to make sure they came to the feast at the Paganeri house! Two hearts melted with longing that the feast must somehow take place! They waited and yearned!

‘Ah! How wonderfully he must have argued!’ Without anyone knowing, a joyous dance was going on in Kalyani’s heart.

“Do you know, Adappa, that your sister Kalyani is going to cook today too?”

“Is that so? Why, Kalyani, is it true?”

Her head nodded “yes”! Her cheeks and eyes tried—and failed—to hide the shyness they held.

“To help Kalyani, I too am going to the kitchen today.”

Everyone burst out laughing. Adappan included!

“Why are you laughing! Kalyani is still a child; what will I do if she comes back from cooking with wounds on her fingers? Not merely on my sister’s finger—not merely on her fingernail—not even that; if so much as the tiniest speck of harm comes to the tip of her nail, I cannot bear it! Isn’t that so, Kalyani!” He goes near his sister and strokes her head.

<!-- source boundary: scan 58 → scan 59 -->

Her eyes become seas with the fullness of her delight.

The kitchen turns into a battlefield of activity! Is it merely because the Pattamangalam people are going to eat there? When Kalyani Nachiyar and, to help her, Vaalukku Veli begin turning the pots and vessels about in the kitchen, can the feet or hands of the cook Kaadai and the cook Kolathari possibly keep up? Can the other servants stand with folded hands and watch?

“Kalyani! The rabbit curry is cooked well! If it stays any longer it will burn! Take it down, Amma!”

“Anna! I’m taking it down now! Ayyo, what a huge pot! I can’t even lift it!”

“Wait! Wait! I’ll take it down!”

“Thangachi! Don’t forget to squeeze lemon into the goat-leg soup!”

“Would I forget? Look, I’ve already kept the lemons ready!”

She cuts the fruit quickly. “Ah!” The knife slices the tip of her finger! Blood oozes out! Blood seems to pour from Vaalukku Veli’s eyes as he rushes over! He is shaken! He puts Kalyani’s injured finger in his mouth, sucks away the seeping blood, tears a strip from the edge of his veshti and winds it around her finger.

Amid tremendous bustle, the deer, rabbits, goats, chickens and other birds that had been transformed into feast dishes in the kitchen seated themselves upon silver

<!-- source boundary: scan 59 → scan 60 -->

plates and came in procession to the dining table.

After all the airs, stubbornness and refusal, after crossing every one of those stages, Vallatharayan of Pattamangalam entered the Paganeri mansion. Vaalukku Veli and Karutha Adappan welcomed him and led him to the dining hall. Seeing Vellai Ayyar arrive with him, the cooks Kaadai and Kolathari looked at one another and laughed.

“Isn’t Saami a vegetarian? Will he eat all this?”

The dining hall looked like the day after a great war!

Vellai Ayyar asked for a leaf to be laid separately for him at the same dining table! If there was vegetarian food, he asked them to serve it! If not, rice! And a little buttermilk with it! Eggs alone he could eat; there was neither flesh nor bone in them! Therefore, his argument was that they belonged among vegetarian foods.

That awaited figure still had not entered the dining hall! With such yearning, two eyes wandered from somewhere in a corner. Karutha Adappan, her elder brother, had seated Vallatharayan and gone outside; the expectation that he would return together with Vairamuthan was doing something indescribable to Kalyani’s heart!

They had met only for a single day—and that too only that morning! Yet she had heard many people, including her own brother, speak of his skill, strength and nature. All those sweet

<!-- source boundary: scan 60 → scan 61 -->

reports had, without her even knowing it, become firm foundation stones for the tower of love that was now to be built!

If he came, should she go and serve him or not? When even her going to work in the kitchen today, something she had never done before, had amazed her brother and everyone else, what would they all think if she now set off to serve the meal too? And not only that—what torment would that mischievous shyness put her through while she served?

What if she poured soup instead of curry, and buttermilk instead of rasam! Chi! Chi! Her honour would be gone! So it would be wiser to stay respectfully aside and drink in his beauty with her eyes!

What is this cowardice? Is she not the sister of a great warrior? Why should she not stand firm in preserving the conduct proper to a Tamil woman and serve food to everyone herself? Everyone else is fine; but when I go before him to serve, will my eyes even see the leaf? And if those magnetic eyes of his seize and pull my eyes toward them, I may stagger and fall right there!

Leave that aside. I am being tossed about amid all this struggle, and I still haven’t seen him! Could he perhaps have gone back to Pattamangalam? Ayyo! Was it for this that I cut my finger? I still haven’t seen him! Kalyani went on suffering in restless longing.
